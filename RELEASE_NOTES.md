# Release notes

## Version 1.1 (build 2)

What's New text for App Store Connect:

```
• Profile photos — add a picture to your account, and see your friends' photos on the SalatStreaks leaderboard.
• Nudge a friend — send a friend a gentle reminder to pray a prayer they haven't completed yet today.
• Prayer calendar — tap anyone on SalatStreaks to see their month at a glance: every day they prayed is marked, with the total at the top.
• All-five badge — a badge appears next to your name when you complete all five daily prayers.
• Your Personal Ayah — the daily verse is now personalized, with a live countdown to when it changes at midnight.
• Accuracy and performance improvements to streaks and the friends list.
```
